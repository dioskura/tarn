Possibly related to Proto-Germanic *turnaz* (bitter) and PIE *der-* (to split, to crack) — though those deeper layers are scholarly maybes, not certainties. I built a cathedral out of the maybes once and Marina caught me. What's solid: small lake, no visible source, fed from underground. That's enough.
